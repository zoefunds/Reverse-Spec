# PEXO_PROMPTS

## Decision: no generative footage
Every scene in this film is real product UI or deterministic Remotion motion graphics. For a verification protocol, conceptual stock-style footage would *weaken* credibility, so the Pexo footage budget is intentionally zero (rule: "No Pexo clip should appear merely because it looks cinematic").

## Audio prompts (used)

### Voiceover (per-scene TTS, neutral voice)
Purpose: narration. Text: exactly the 8 lines in VOICEOVER_SCRIPT.md, one file per scene for frame-accurate sync. Delivery: calm, confident, measured, ~135 wpm, slight lift on "ReverseSpec" and the final line. No music, no effects, clean studio quality, mono, 48 kHz.

### Music bed
Purpose: single evolving underscore. Prompt: "Minimal electronic underscore for a technical product film, 90 seconds: quiet tense pulse (0–15 s), a restrained lift with a soft synth motif (15–22 s), steady mid-tempo momentum with subtle percussion (22–58 s), confident wide pads (58–84 s), resolving to a single sustained closing note (84–90 s). No vocals, no risers-per-second, no cinematic booms, no EDM drop. Dark, precise, premium. Instrumental, 48 kHz stereo."
