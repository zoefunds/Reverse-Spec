# VIDEO_BRIEF — ReverseSpec Bounties launch film

- **One-sentence product definition:** ReverseSpec is a bounty protocol on GenLayer where the spec is a starting hypothesis — AI-validator consensus pays the solver who proves they solved the *deeper* problem behind it.
- **Target viewer:** GenLayer hackathon reviewers, web3/AI-infra developers, and technically literate founders/investors.
- **Primary video objective:** Hackathon / community presentation film that makes reviewers believe the build is real, deep, and native to GenLayer.
- **Central message:** Subjective, money-moving verdicts ("you ignored the spec but solved the real problem") can be adjudicated trustlessly — by validators that fetch and judge real evidence inside consensus.
- **Intended emotion:** Sharp curiosity → technical respect → confidence.
- **Current product stage:** Working MVP, live on GenLayer **StudioNet** (test network). Deployed frontend (Vercel), 24/7 backend (Fly.io), deployed contract `0x79F6…D2C4`.
- **Implemented capabilities:** 1,477-line Intelligent Contract (22 methods, 27 direct tests); payable GEN escrow; contract-side evidence fetch via GenVM web access; real consensus verdicts (DEEP_SOLUTION composite 78 / depth 91; REDEFINING depth 100); finalize paid 47,500 GEN; working claim_rewards (native transfers); on-chain conservation invariant; wallet auth; indexer read-model; 10-route Next.js frontend.
- **Simulated capabilities:** None in-product — but the *bounties themselves* are demonstration bounties created by the team during E2E testing, and the network is StudioNet, not a production mainnet.
- **Future capabilities:** Mainnet deployment, organic bounty market, dispute depth beyond current tiers.
- **Principal call to action:** "Explore the live app — reverse-spec.vercel.app".
- **Final video duration:** 99 s master @ 30 fps (extended from the 90s target because the real generated voiceover for scene 2 ran 17.8s of natural speech; every other scene held to or beat its budget).
- **Output aspect ratios:** 16:9 master (1920×1080), 9:16 (1080×1920), 1:1 (1080×1080). Optional teaser deferred (see unresolved list).
